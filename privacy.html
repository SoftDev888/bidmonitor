BidMonitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BidMonitor is a private tool that helps a small team of job seekers and their manager keep track of job applications: the replies employers send, next steps, and interview schedules. It is not a public service and is used only by people the operator invites. This policy explains what BidMonitor does with data from a connected Google account.

What BidMonitor accesses

When a job seeker connects their Gmail, Google asks them to allow these permissions. BidMonitor uses each one only as described:

  • Gmail: view and modify, but not delete (gmail.modify). BidMonitor reads the sender, subject and text of new messages to find the ones about the person's job applications (application confirmations, replies, interview invitations, assessments, offers, rejections). The only change it makes to a message is the read / unread mark, and only when the person or their manager clicks “Mark read” or “Mark unread”. It never deletes, archives, labels or moves any message.
  • Gmail: send email (gmail.send). Used only to send a reply that a person wrote or reviewed and then sent by clicking “Send”. Nothing is ever sent automatically.
  • Google Calendar: read only (calendar.readonly). Used to find interview events (time, time zone, meeting link, attendees) so interviews can be shown and reminded. BidMonitor never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.
  • Basic account information (email address). Used to confirm that the connected account is the person's job-search account.

What is stored, and what is not

  • Messages that are about job applications are stored: sender, subject, text, date, and a short summary and category (for example “interview”, “rejection”), plus interview details found in them.
  • All other messages are not stored. For them BidMonitor keeps only the message ID, so it does not look at them twice.
  • Calendar events that are not interviews are not stored.
  • The Google permission (OAuth refresh token) is stored encrypted. BidMonitor never sees or stores a Google password.

How the data is used

Only to show the job seeker and their manager the replies to their applications, the next steps, and their interview schedule, to help them write replies (an AI writing assistant drafts text that a person reviews before sending), and to warn about problems with the account (for example Google security notices or bounced emails).

AI processing

To sort job-related emails (for example “interview” or “rejection”), to pick out interview times, and to draft replies, the text of those job-related messages is sent to Anthropic (the Claude AI model) for processing. Anthropic acts only as a service provider for this purpose. The sender and subject of other messages are checked by BidMonitor itself and are not sent to Anthropic. Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

Data is visible only to the job seeker and their manager within BidMonitor, and is processed by Anthropic as described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone else, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

BidMonitor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping the account safe

  • Mailboxes are checked about every 10 minutes, only for new messages, and less often whenever Google asks.
  • Replies are limited per day, and BidMonitor stops all checking and sending for an account when it sees signs of a problem (security notices, blocked or bounced emails, unusual sending) until the manager has reviewed it.

Removing access and deleting data

  • The job seeker can click Disconnect in BidMonitor at any time, or remove “BidMonitor” at myaccount.google.com/permissions. BidMonitor then stops accessing the account.
  • Stored job-related emails are kept while the account is connected, so the job seeker and their manager can follow the application. To have them deleted, contact the operator below; they are deleted within 30 days.
