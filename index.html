BidMonitor

A private job-application tracker for a small, invited team.

BidMonitor helps job seekers and their manager keep their job search organised:

  • which applications got a reply, and which are still waiting;
  • next steps the employer asked for (questions, documents, availability);
  • interview invitations and schedules, with reminders before each interview;
  • help writing replies, which a person always reviews and sends.

With the job seeker's permission, BidMonitor reads their Gmail and Google Calendar to find job-related emails and interviews. It never deletes or moves email and never sends anything without a person clicking Send. Job-related emails are sorted, and replies drafted, with the help of Anthropic's Claude AI. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses and stores.

BidMonitor is not a public service; access is by invitation only.
